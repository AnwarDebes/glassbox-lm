# Research notes: ideas, literature and design

This folder holds what comes before the next study: the ideas the programme started from, what a literature search found about each of them, the design that came out of it, and the list of open work. Nothing in this folder has been run. Every expectation in it is an estimate until an experiment of a study says otherwise. The studies themselves, with their runs and results, are in [../studies](../studies/README.md).

| Page | Content |
|---|---|

| [board/](board/README.md) | The writing-board pages the ideas come from: 27 photos and a short reading of each of the 32 pages. |

| [roadmap.md](roadmap.md) | Open work: what the studies registered or prepared and did not run, and the next steps of the design, in order. |

## How far to trust these pages

- The literature search was made on 2026-10-10. The claims that the findings rest on were checked a second time against their sources: 225 were confirmed, 66 corrected, 2 refuted, and 24 could not be checked. The pages use the corrected versions.
- Most papers were read as an abstract or a web page and not in full. A paper that was not read is marked so.
- "None found" means not found in a few searches. It never means that the thing does not exist.
- A statement about a study of this repository repeats the results page of that study, which names the run and gives the qualifiers.
- The expected numbers of the design are estimates from published tables. The first experiments of the design have run in the study tally (the final runs of T0, and smoke, timing, baseline and exploratory runs of B1, B6, A1, A2 and C3); none of them has a verdict yet.
