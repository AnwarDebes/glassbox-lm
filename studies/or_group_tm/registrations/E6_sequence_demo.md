Written before any run. Exploratory; the data is synthetic and is generated here with fixed seeds.

## Why

The upstream package's newest example (examples/SequenceDemo.py at commit f40b2a3, read today) is a synthetic form of slide 10: a sequence of 6 positions holds a permutation of 6 symbols, each symbol is written with one of 3 interchangeable signs ("synonyms"), 108 input bits (position x symbol x sign), and the class is the parity of the permutation. One word order has 3^6 = 729 sign variants; 10,000 training sequences cover about 14 per order. A clause that is an AND over the 6 positions of an OR over the 3 signs of the right symbol covers all 729 variants of one order.

## Question

Trained on 10,000 sequences, does the OR-group rule build such clauses (one clause per word order, all three signs in every group), and how does accuracy hold when the signs are Zipf-skewed in training and the skew is reversed at test (the demo's synonym drift)?

## Data (generated by `src/run_seq.py`, the demo's construction with a seeded generator)

Length 6, 3 signs, 10,000 train and 10,000 test sequences, label = number of inversions modulo 2 (as the demo). Settings: (a) uniform orders and signs; (b) sign skew 1.0 (Zipf exponent over the 3 signs) without drift; (c) sign skew 1.0 with the test signs reversed (drift); (d) order skew 1.0 with reversed orders at test. Data seeds 1 to 3.

## Model, fixed for the first look

6 sides (one per position), 1 group per position, 18 bits per position, clauses of positive polarity voting for label 1; `--mode atleast --min-true 2` (a clause that matches at least 2 positions learns the signs it lacks), reinforce critical, reject weakest, r 2, initial state -1, one seed event per example, T 1, 1,000 clauses, 20 passes. Arms that may follow, each logged: mode near; min-true 3; 4 clauses (the demo's count); T 2.

## Scores

Test accuracy (predict 1 when a clause fires), share of test sequences of label 1 that some clause covers, accuracy on test sequences whose exact bit pattern is not in the training data, mean precision and recall of the clauses that fire (the demo's two clause scores), clauses used, signs per group. References computed on the same data: a memoriser (label of the identical training pattern, else 0) and logistic regression on the 108 bits.

## Bar

Setting (a): test accuracy at least 0.95 on 3 of 3 data seeds. Settings (b) to (d): reported without a bar. A miss on (a) is reported as it is.

## Limits, stated now

The upstream demo's own numbers are not known to me at this point (a separate job runs it); the comparison with the package is made only on identical data files and is added afterwards. The task is synthetic and closely related to the upstream author's unpublished work in progress: any result here is a replication on his task with a different rule, not a new idea about the task. Clauses of this rule do not share groups, so a sign never seen with a word order in training cannot be covered for that order.
