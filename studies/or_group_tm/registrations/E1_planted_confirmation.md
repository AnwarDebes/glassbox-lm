# E1: confirmation of the OR-group rule on planted-group worlds of new seeds (frozen before its runs)

Publication note: the text below is the plan as it was fixed on 2026-11-12T15:58:27Z (SHA-256 of that file: `ed616c4dc66cae4458b7a225aec20f4354572af60e2158e30ba4972ae317e5f7`), with folder and file names changed to those of this repository. Rules, bars, settings and numbers are unchanged.

## Question

Does the rule of E0b (mode `near`, one seed event per example) meet the bars of Check 2a of the hierarchical_tm study on worlds it was not designed on?

## What I knew at the freeze (all read from `logs/RUNS.md`)

- E0 (first rule, seeds 1 to 3 of the study's files): bar NOT met by any gating variant (plain accuracy 0.68 to 0.82, recovery 0.125 to 0.625), purity 1.0 for `near` / `side`.
- E0b (post hoc revision: one seed event per example), T 4, 5 passes: L2 plain accuracy 1.0000 / 1.0000 / 1.0000 and recovery 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.0; L3 0.9998 / 1.0000 / 1.0000, recovery 1.0 / 1.0 / 0.875, unseen pairs 0.9998 / 1.0000 / 1.0000.
- Design arms after E0b, same files, 5 passes: T 8 and T 16 give recovery 1.0 on all six worlds; coverage of all groups rises from 0.74 to 0.89 (T 4) to 0.96 to 1.0 (T 16). T 16 is chosen for E1 on this ground.
- No run on any world of seeds 11 to 15 exists; the smoke test uses seed 990.

## Data

Worlds generated by the study's `pcp_world.World(rung, seed)` (file pinned in the freeze record) for rungs L2 and L3 and the NEW seeds 11, 12, 13, 14, 15: 10,000 train, 4,000 dev and (L3) 4,000 unseen pairs, with `run_pcp.py --fresh`. The model seed equals the world seed.

## Setting, fixed

`--mode near --max-seed 1 --T 16 --clauses 40 --K 1 --init-state -1 --seed-state 0 --r 2 --passes 5`, all clauses of positive polarity, all probabilities 1.0. Ten runs: 2 rungs x 5 seeds. Nothing is tuned on these worlds; a run is not repeated unless it crashes (a crash and its rerun are logged).

## Rules (bars of Check 2a, gates.md of the study; 5 seeds in place of 3)

- R1 (L2): on at least 4 of 5 seeds, plain dev accuracy (vote sum at least 1) at least 0.90 AND group recovery at least 0.80 (the study's `pcp_world.recovery`).
- R2 (L3): the same on at least 4 of 5 seeds.
- R3 (L3, unseen word pairs): plain accuracy on the unseen pairs at least 0.85 on at least 4 of 5 seeds.
- Reported without a rule: tuned accuracy, AUC, purity and coverage of all groups, seconds per pass, every seed.

No multiplicity correction; each rule stands alone. E1 is met only if R1, R2 and R3 are all met.

## Reference (stock rule, the study's seeds 1 to 3, from its results page)

L2 at 100 passes: plain accuracy 0.523 / 0.522 / 0.572, recovery 0.625 / 0.5 / 0.75; L3 (s 800, 300 passes): accuracy 0.8365 / 0.821 / 0.821, recovery 0 / 0 / 0, unseen pairs 0.788 / 0.74 / 0.752 (tuned). The stock rule has not been run on seeds 11 to 15; a head-to-head on these worlds would be a separate, later job and no E1 rule depends on it.

## What a miss means

Any of R1 to R3 missed: reported as it is; the rule is then "works on its design worlds only" and nothing further is built on the planted tests.

## Not claimed whatever the outcome

Nothing about real text or synonyms; the worlds are artificial and the rule was designed on worlds of the same generator. No "first" or "new" before a literature check. The hidden groups are recovered from pair labels; this is not unsupervised clustering of the words.
