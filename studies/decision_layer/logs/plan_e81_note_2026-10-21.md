## The driver

- **logs/e81_driver.sh** (73e44676d4dba0eb135905d00e858eff31beb6b683a2780ca8dedf5156d97ac7) runs the frozen job files in
  the plan's order, each through scripts/seq_queue.sh (one job at a time, the GPU lock, the CPU gate of 4.5):
  1. logs/jobs_e81_smoke.txt;
  2. `scripts/e81_pick.py --stage smoke`, saved to logs/e81_smoke_check.out;
  3. logs/jobs_e81a.txt;
  4. `--stage screen`, saved to logs/e81_pick_screen.out (it writes logs/jobs_e81b.txt);
  5. logs/jobs_e81b.txt, if it has any job;
  6. `--stage final`, saved to logs/e81_pick_final.out.
- **When a check fails:** the driver stops there and logs it.
- **No other logic:** it makes no choice of its own.
- **The checker runs** on the CPU: `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=` empty, 1 thread, nice 19.
- **The overrides:** E81_ROOT, E81_QUEUE and E81_PY exist only for the test below. The real run sets none of them.
- **Start:** by hand, only after the "3c done" note of the text-understanding line, alone on the GPU:
  `nohup bash logs/e81_driver.sh > logs/e81_driver.log 2>&1 &`. The PID is saved, and only that PID is ever signalled.
