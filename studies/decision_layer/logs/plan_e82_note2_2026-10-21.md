  - the fit filter (below).
- **The gaps are small:** at the dev-picked best epoch they are inside the 0.3-0.5 seed noise, and STAR's seed 2 alone

## What the first note left out

- **The sharing:** the decision to share the GPU (01:28 UTC) came before the freeze (01:31:09 UTC), but it
  was not written into the plan, whose line says "after step 3c of the text-understanding line".
  - Jobs 1 to 3 ran beside 3c. Job 3 overlapped from 01:41:06 until 3c stopped itself at 01:43:09 UTC.
  - The TM's arithmetic is exact and its random stream depends only on the seed, so the sharing changes speed, not
    results. All 12 saved models reproduce their rows exactly (the integrity part of the review).
- **The 01:45 decision** to resume was taken while ABCD's +0.56 (jobs 1-2) was known. The decision does not depend on
  outcomes, because the frozen sample is kept whole.
- **The throttling:** the queue log shows throttled periods before jobs 2 and 3 (3 of 100 periods at 01:41:06). The
  sharing rule counted that as a pause trigger. The rows are unaffected.
- **Job 3's exit line:** job 3 (MultiWOZ seed 1) has no exit line in logs/e82_queue.log, because its runner was
  stopped. Its log, row and model are complete.
- **e82_check.py** checks the table hash on seed 1's row only, and not the MANIFEST, which the plan promised. The
  integrity part of the review checked every candidate row and the MANIFEST, and they hold.
