# e71 v2: second dated note (2026-10-19): step 2 on a fixed sample of 50, then seeds 2 and 3

This adds to `logs/plan_e71_v2_amendment.md` (frozen 2026-10-19T03:30:28Z) and to the first dated note
(`logs/plan_e71_v2_note_2026-10-19.md`). It is a deviation from the frozen order (step 2, then seeds 2 to 5 "as the
budget allows"). It was decided at 10:17 UTC with conditions (1) to (4) below. It is frozen with
`scripts/freeze.py` before any further MILP run.

**Publication note:** the wording of this copy was edited for publication on 2026-10-23. The registered question, rules, thresholds, seeds, analysis plans, amendments, dates and times are unchanged; the freeze record next to this file holds the SHA-256 of the text as registered.

## Why

- **The probe evidence** (tag e71v2_budget_probe, before the v2 freeze; 4 probe instances, 120 s each):
  - k = 1 was certified on 4 of 4 (14.6 to 53.8 s);
  - k = 2, 3 and 5 were unknown at the time limit on 4 of 4.
  - So step 2 (k = 3 and 5 by MILP for every pair certified at k = 2) will most likely return "unknown".
- **The budget:** 512 pairs are certified at k = 2. At 120 s each, k = 3 alone is about 61,440 s (17.1 h). The 24 h
  budget, counted from 02:10 UTC, ends at 02:10 UTC on 2026-10-20, and at the switch (10:18 UTC) 15.9 h are left.
  - The full step 2 would use the whole budget and leave none for step 3.
  - Step 3 (exhaustive k = 1, 2 on another TM seed) took about 6.7 h for seed 1.
- **Exhaustive certificates on 3 models are worth more than about 512 unknowns**.

## The stopped run

The full step 2 (tag e71v2_k35, `verify_enum.py --check milp35`) started at 10:16 UTC. It was stopped by PID (TERM)
at 10:18:03 UTC, during its first MILP, and had written 0 rows. Nothing ran "before the switch".

- **The population:** the 512 (state, group) pairs certified at k = 2 for TM seed 1 (tag e71v2), sorted by
  (state, group).
- **The draw:** `numpy.random.default_rng(0).choice(512, 50, replace=False)`, with the indices sorted.
- **The file:** `logs/e71_k35_sample.json`, sha256 5a42edcf25522aa28a501c4b4d25d72d4725411174a1a73c326a011000da39ff.
- **Composition:** 44 states. By group: log-out-in 14, enter-details 9, search-faq 9, P_first 8, verify-identity 8,

## The runs, in order (one process at a time, 2 threads)

1. **k = 3 and 5 on the sample** (`scripts/e71_k35_sample.py --seed 1`, tag e71v2_k35):
   - the same call as the frozen milp35 mode (verify_local.solve_group, budget = k, 120 s);
   - k = 5 only where k = 3 is certified (condition 3);
   - a sampled pair that already has a row is not rerun (condition 2);
   - rows carry "sampled": true and the sample's hash.
   - The frozen milp35 mode's resume would have gone on to k = 5 after a non-certified k = 3 row. The wrapper does
     not, and that path never ran.
   - At most 50 x 2 x 120 s = 3.3 h; about 1.7 h if k = 3 is mostly unknown.
2. **Exhaustive k = 1 and 2 for TM seed 2, then seed 3** (`verify_enum.py --check run --seed <s> --tag e71v2`; the
   fixed 99ba6582):
   - the same 200 states (sample_states, rng 0, independent of the seed) and the same rules;
   - about 6.7 h each.
3. **Seeds 4 and 5** are reported as not run (budget). If the budget ends during seed 3, its finished states are
   reported with their count, as partial.
