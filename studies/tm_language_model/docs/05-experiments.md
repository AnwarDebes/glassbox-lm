# 05 Experiments

Each spec: name, purpose, dataset, config, command, metrics, expected runtime/memory on 32 GB, success threshold, failure meaning, figure. E0..E11 are the initial design's experiments, corrected; s1..s4 and the causal perplexity protocol CP are new (design decisions). Added on 2026-09-22 (docs/12 F): s5 clause-shard equivalence, CM the cost model, TB the equal-cost transformer placement, H1 to H3 the proposal rows. Thresholds for every experiment that feeds a claim are pre-registered in docs/13. Milestones refer to docs/04.

Conventions: there is no system Python, so every command runs inside `.venv-tm` (or `.venv-dl` for neural baselines) through `scripts/run_logged.py` and the GPU lock (`scripts/gpu_run.sh`), which write `results/<timestamp>_<name>/`. Runtime and memory figures in this file are [unverified] estimates until a log replaces them. Track A always passes `grid=(16*13,1,1)`. All tuning uses validation data; every test-set access is counted (docs/06).

## E0 Output-count scaling (M1)
Purpose: measure class-loop cost on STOCK GraphTM. Dataset: trivial two-node graphs. Config: clauses 2000, depth 1, HV 256, outputs {10,100,1000,5000,16000}. Command: `python experiments/p0_scaling_harness.py`. Metrics: examples/s, with repeats and variance (shared host). Runtime: minutes; memory < 2 GB. Success: monotone curve captured. Failure: flat slope means class loop is not the bottleneck (kill criterion). Figure: F1 scaling curve.

## E1 Masked-token sanity on stock GraphTM (M1)
Dataset: 5M-token subset, vocab 2000. Config: clauses 4000, depth {1,2,3} (the initial design's depth 2 is the reference point), HV 512, message 256, mask_rate 0.15, ONE target per graph, s swept over {10, 30, 100, 300, 1000, 3000} together with `max_included_literals` (the frequency trap, docs/02). Metrics: masked-token top-1 vs unigram, left bigram, right bigram, bidirectional n-gram. Runtime: hours per run; memory < 8 GB. Grid order (a time mechanism, not a cut; docs/12 F7): the full s sweep at depth 2 first (the initial design's reference point), then depth 1 and depth 3 at the two best s values; if the depth ordering differs between those two s values the remaining cells are run too. Success: beats the left bigram. Failure: stop and diagnose. Figure: none (gate).

## E2 Binomial-draw equivalence and speedup (M5)
Metrics: distributional test pass (exact Binomial reference, not the initial design's Poisson(Q); Q-gate selections counted with the error gate forced open; docs/02); measured examples/s vs dense at Q in {1.0,0.5,0.1}. Success: test passes and speedup at least 2x at Q=1. Failure: keep dense; drop the sparse-draw headline. Figure: F2 speedup vs Q.

## E3 WikiText-103 masked-token (M6, Gate 1)
Dataset: WikiText-103 raw, BPE vocab 8k and 16k. Main config (Track B, MASKED training): clauses C from 1M to 16M, mask_rate 0.15, T=64, s=16 defaults then swept on validation. Reference row (Track A, patched GraphTM, V=8k only): clauses 8192, depth 3, HV 2048, message 1024, mask_rate 0.15, T and s swept, Q swept, time-boxed at 72 GPU-h. Command: `python experiments/e3_wikitext_masked.py --vocab 8000`. Metrics: top-1, top-5 on the FROZEN eval mask sets (subword and whole-word variants reported separately) vs left bigram and in-house CBOW-kNN (the initial design's kNN-over-GloVe is replaced, docs/12); full-training wall time against the 48 h Gate 2 target. Order: validation first, then test once. Success: beats both baselines. Failure: kill criterion (bigram). Figure: F3 top-1/top-5 bars.

## E4 Full WikiText-103 within 48 h (Gate 2: wall time in M6, ECE in M8)
Adds absorbing states (crystallization), insertion-probability sampling, incremental decode, all on Track B. Metrics: wall time, ECE. Success: under 48 h and ECE < 5%. Figure: F4 crystallization (G8).

## E5 Frozen clause-code transfer (M9)
The same mechanism as the greedy layer 2 of H2 (fired-clause bits as frozen features), applied to external classification sets. Dataset: R8 (`dxgp/R8`), IMDB (`stanfordnlp/imdb`). Use clause codes (the fired-clause vector; `transform()` on Track A) as frozen features; classifier is a linear head or a small TM. Baselines: from-scratch TM (R8 96.16), TM-GloVe 97.50, Clusters 97.92 (teacher-dependent) [stated]; the from-scratch TM is re-measured in-house. Success: gain over from-scratch. Failure: report negative with ablations. Figure: F5.

## E6 Coherence per tier (M9)
Tier 1 of this task (same document vs different document, two paragraphs) is exactly the slide-11 task of the supervisor's project proposal (unpublished), so E6 tier 1 is the dataset of H1 and H2 and is measured on every row of that table. Tier 4 (counterfactually edited positives) is IN SCOPE since 2026-09-22: `third_party/ref/tm-llm-robustness-benchmark` supplies the counterfactual IMDb pairs (Kaushik et al. 2020, 488 samples) and the harness. Metrics: accuracy on tiers 1 to 4. Success: the hardest tier well above chance. Figure: F6.

## E7 Negation headline (G3; smoke run in M6, full in M9)
Datasets: minimal pairs and NaN-NLI (`joey234/nan-nli`) with sensitivity AND correctness; CondaQA (`lasha-nlp/CONDAQA`; consistency; human 81.58%) run ONCE and reported honestly. Baseline: BERT-base. Include the slide-10 pair as a worked example. Polarity symbols on/off is an ablation computed from MASKED text only. Success: TM sensitivity above BERT on at least one axis (existence claim); the robustness ratio is reported either way. Figure: F7.

## E10 Energy per token (G4, M9)

## s1 Clause scaling (M7)
Purpose: the capacity curve. Accuracy and cost vs clause count. Dataset: WikiText-103, BPE V in {8k, 16k} (32k is first to go). Config: Track B, C from 8k upward by factors of 4 to the largest count that fits 32 GB (64M clauses at about 290 B per clause is 18.4 GB [derived, docs/03]; the reached maximum is stated with every claim), MASKED and CAUSAL trainings, 3 seeds at the small sizes, single-seed at the top and labelled so. Plotted log-log with one line per seed (docs/13, section 5). Metrics: top-1/top-5, bits per byte, examples/s, peak GPU memory, candidates per example, fired clauses per example. Success: a measured curve with variance; no threshold on its shape. Failure meaning: > 5,000 candidates per example at C=1M triggers KB4 (pair anchors, then cap cluster-anchored clauses). Also in this experiment: the wide-vote tier on/off and the insertion-probability ablation (T2.2). Figure: F12 accuracy and cost vs C.

## s2 Context-length scaling (M7)
Purpose: the huge-context claim, as a measurement. Dataset: WikiText-103, document-bounded contexts. Config: Track B at a FIXED clause budget, context N from 16 to 65k tokens (bag buckets enabled up to N), plus the pure-bag "pointer clause" ablation. Metrics: measured cost per token (training and streaming inference) vs N, top-1 and perplexity vs N. Success: the cost-vs-N curve is logged; the target (not a result) is near-constant cost per token. Failure meaning: KB5, bags add < 0.3 points top-1 and < 2% perplexity at N=4096: report the negative result and keep the cost-vs-N curve. The whole range to 65k is run (nothing is cut, docs/12 F4); the pre-registered target is at most 2x cost per token at N=65536 vs N=16 (docs/13, H3.3). Figure: F13 cost per token and accuracy vs N, log-log.

## s3 Batch staleness (M3; repeated on the GPU in M4 if B changes)
Purpose: batch-synchronous TA feedback has no published analysis (docs/01). Dataset: tiny LM (5M tokens, V=2000). Config: B in {1, 64, 256, 1024, 4096}, everything else fixed, clause-sequential application, cap 256 events per clause per batch. Metrics: top-1 vs B, clauses recruited per batch, throughput vs B. Success: a default B within 0.5 points of B=1. Failure meaning: KB3, B=1024 loses > 1 point vs B=1: default B = largest within 0.5 points; if < 64, micro-batch and re-derive throughput. Figure: F14 accuracy vs B.

## s4 Deviation ladder (M3)
Purpose: price every departure from the published coalesced TM. Dataset: NoisyXOR and one multi-class task for parity; the tiny LM for the ladder. Config: D0 is the dense-equivalent mode of R8 (all literals pre-tracked, random +/-1 votes, uniform Binomial negatives, eager decay, index off, B=1), which must match tmu CoTM; D7 is the full default engine; each step D1..D7 flips one flag (the flag order is fixed in docs/11 at M3). Also reports `count_probe`: what plain counting achieves in the same literal space. Metrics: top-1 per rung, delta per flag, throughput per rung. Success: D0 within 2 points of tmu (else KB1); the tiny LM beats the bigram and reaches at least 70% of count-probe top-1 (else KB2). Figure: F15 ladder.

## CP Causal perplexity protocol (M7)
Purpose: the first LM perplexity for a logic-native model, as a target, reported so that it cannot be misread.
- Model: Track B, CAUSAL training (left segments only, every position a target). Probabilities from the calibrated `logprobs` (two scalars fitted on validation NLL). Probabilities must sum to one over the full subword vocabulary; assert it.
- Primary metric: bits per byte on the WikiText-103 raw test set (total negative log2-likelihood of the subword sequence divided by the UTF-8 byte count of the same text). Bits per byte is tokenizer-independent.
- Secondary metric: per-word perplexity, converted from the same total log-likelihood divided by the word count, LABELLED "open-vocabulary, converted, not like-for-like with published closed-vocabulary numbers".
- The ONLY like-for-like comparator is the in-house interpolated Kneser-Ney 5-gram on identical tokens (same BPE, same splits, same byte counts), itself validated against NLTK on a toy corpus (`test_kn_vs_nltk.py`). No KN or neural perplexity from the literature is quoted as a comparator.
- Diffusion ELBO perplexity (E4/M8) is an upper bound and is compared only against this causal TM perplexity for the initial design's 2x kill criterion.
- Failure meaning: KB7, sparse-only causal perplexity > 1.5x KN-5: enable the wide tier [superseded by docs/12 F19]; still above KN-5: report perplexity as a first measurement with no superiority claim.
- Test split touched once, after all choices are frozen on validation. 3-seed variance at small sizes. Report the contamination overlap (docs/06) next to the number.

## TK Tokenization-level ablation (M3, tiny LM; added 2026-09-22)
Purpose: RDDLGN found subword tokens far worse than word-level tokens for a logic model (11.08% vs 23.28%); know early whether the engine shares that penalty. Dataset: the tiny LM slice. Config: BPE V=2000 vs word-level V=2000 with <unk> (identical text, identical positions counted), same clause budget, 5 seeds. Metrics: top-1 on frozen masks (subword and whole-word variants), bits per byte, clauses owned per class. Success: none pre-registered beyond "reported before the M6 run"; the headline stays BPE 8k as in docs/13, and the report shows this ablation next to it. Figure: F20.

## s5 Clause-shard equivalence (M3 oracle, M4 GPU)
Purpose: the mechanism a multi-device run would use, proved bit-exact at small scale (pillar P3, docs/07). Config: the same tiny LM run three ways: unsharded; 2 shards; 4 shards, with the exchanges of docs/11 section 9 and nothing else. On the oracle the shards are separate `OracleEngine` instances with an explicit exchange step; on the GPU they are separate engine instances on the one V100. Metrics: `state_hash` of the concatenated shard states vs the unsharded hash after 200 steps (oracle) and 100 steps at B in {64, 1024} (GPU); `DIAG_COUNTERS` equal; bytes exchanged per batch logged and compared with the bound of docs/11 section 9. Success: equality. Failure meaning: docs/11 section 9 is incomplete; the spec is fixed and the test rerun; no multi-device statement is made until it passes. Figure: none (a pass/fail table).

## CM Cost model with measured constants (M4, M7)
Purpose: the constants behind the bounds of docs/11 section 10, so that the cost of any (C, N, V) inside the measured range can be computed and the range itself is stated. Measured under the exclusive GPU lock, median of at least 5 repeats with the min to max range: bytes per clause (from peak GPU memory vs C); ns per candidate, ns per verified literal, ns per fired clause, ns per feedback event, sweep ns per clause (from `DIAG_COUNTERS` and CUDA events over s1 and s2 runs); ns per token vs N with bags at each bucket count; the same on the 6-CPU engine. Output: a table of constants with log paths and a formula `ns_per_token(C, N) = a + b * candidates(C) + c * fired(C) + d * log2(N)` fitted on the measured points, with the fit residuals. Any use of the formula outside the measured range is labelled [derived] and drawn dashed. Figure: F16 measured vs fitted cost.

## TB Equal-cost transformer placement (M7)
Purpose: pillar P1's neural comparator, at equal cost rather than at a fixed GPU-hour cap. Model: a from-scratch causal transformer in `.venv-dl` (decoder-only, the same BPE tokens, the same document boundaries, the same test bytes), sized by a small validation sweep over {2, 4, 6} layers at width {128, 256}. Two budgets, both measured under the exclusive GPU lock on the same V100: (a) the same wall-clock as the reported causal TM run; (b) the same joules as that run (NVML, idle subtracted). The 24 GPU-h capped masked transformer of docs/06 is kept as a third row. Metrics: bpb on the test set, per-word perplexity (labelled converted), joules per token at inference, both directions reported. No threshold (docs/13, H1.3). Figure: F17 bpb vs joules for TM, KN-5 and the transformer rows.

## H1 The proposal's mechanism, measured: hierarchical TM on the slide-11 task (M9)
Purpose: answer the project proposal with a number. Task: the slide-11 task of the project proposal, balanced, pairs built inside one split only, frozen and hashed (docs/13 section 4). This is E6 tier 1. Vocabulary: BPE V=8000 (reduced vocabulary so that the package fits; docs/02 V3 envelope). Model: `PyHierarchicalTsetlinMachineCUDA.MultiClassTsetlinMachine` with the slide-11 hierarchy `((AND_GROUP, features), (OR_ALTERNATIVES, alternatives), (AND_ALTERNATIVES, concepts), (AND_GROUP, 2))`, `append_negated=False`, `boost_true_positive_feedback=0`, `number_of_state_bits=8`, grid `(16*13,1,1)`, seeded; alternatives in {10, 20}, concepts in {5, 10}, clauses in {1000, 2000}, T and s swept on validation. Control rows on the same pairs: a flat coalesced TM (tmu, bag-of-words of the two slots, no hierarchy) so that the hierarchy's own contribution is measured; a logistic regression on the same bag. Metrics: pair accuracy, validation then test once, 5 seeds, wall time, peak GPU memory, printed hierarchy of the best model. Success: above chance by more than the seed range (docs/13, H4.1). Failure meaning: reported as measured; the row stays. Figure: F18 the proposal table.

## H2 Engine layer 1 and greedy layer 2 on the same task and on the LM (M9)
Purpose: the hybrid of docs/03 (the HTM answer): the engine's self-supervised clauses as layer 1, a TM over their fired bits as layer 2, trained greedily (layer 1 frozen), which is the stacking idea of the project proposal in the only form the HGTM experiments found trainable [stated: the HGTM repository reports that joint two-layer training collapsed to chance and greedy layer-wise training worked on a toy task]. Rows on the H1 pairs: (a) layer 1 = fired-clause bits of the trained masked engine over each paragraph (union over positions, and the per-position mean), read by a logistic regression; (b) layer 1 bits read by the engine itself in tabular mode (docs/11 section 6) as layer 2; (c) row (b) with a second greedy layer over layer-2 bits. On the LM: (d) layer 2 in tabular mode over the layer-1 fired bits at the target position, predicting the token, vs layer 1 alone, on the tiny LM (M3 scale) and, if the M6 run is done, at V=8k. Metrics: pair accuracy and masked top-1 deltas, 5 seeds, paired one-sided Wilcoxon (docs/13, H4.2 and H4.3), printed layer-2 clauses (a layer-2 clause reads `IF clause_17 AND NOT clause_903 THEN same_document`). Success: the pre-registered deltas. Failure meaning: negative result with the delta and its interval; the engine's layer 1 stays the model. Figure: F18 (same table), F19 layer-2 clause printout.

## H3 Multi-view masked LM with a clause fusion head (M9, optional, last)
Purpose: the Multi-HGTM idea [stated: the repository AnwarDebes/Multi-HGTM, where a clause fusion head beat late fusion on a synthetic cross-view task and gave no gain on real data], as an ablation on the engine. Views: left segments only, right segments only, cluster-symbol segments only; each a separately trained engine; fusion = a tabular-mode TM over the concatenated fired bits, vs the single full engine. Dropping the right view is the causal mode. Metrics: masked top-1 on the tiny LM, 5 seeds. Success: none pre-registered beyond "reported". Runs only after every other M9 item has a log.
