## Convergence theory
All read in full on 2026-09-22: IDENTITY/NOT 2007.14268 (a single clause converges; s lets it "capture arbitrarily rare patterns"); XOR 2101.02547 (two clauses suffice; T guides which sub-pattern each clause captures); AND/OR 2109.09488 (OR sub-patterns can share one clause); Zhang, Jiao, Granmo, ICLR 2026: Lemma 4 "T ≤ floor(m/2)" for multi-sub-pattern classes, Theorem 9 "T ≤ floor(m/e)" with irrelevant bits, Theorem 3 label noise makes the TM non-absorbing yet learning stays efficient (Remark 4), Theorems 4, 5, 8 irrelevant variables do not prevent convergence, and the practical insight that irrelevant literals accumulate when many clauses are used, so clause length should be limited. Consequences for the engine: docs/11 section 8, questions 5 and 6; the planted-rules test runs noise-free first. Generalized PCL 2310.02005 [verified: exists]. Nothing for autoencoding, hierarchy, or sequence.

### The safe claim
First token-level TM language model trained at corpus scale with a subword vocabulary, evaluated as a generative model with a reported likelihood (masked and causal). Never shortened to "first TM language model", "first TM text generation", "first integer-only language model" or "first logic-native perplexity". The engine's novelty is stated as the COMBINATION: whole-vocabulary position-bound literals with an anchor index, sparse signed integer votes, lazy owned-class decay, multi-scale bag literals for long context, exact counter-based replay, and clause sharding; sparse literals and position-tagged context features are prior art (Sparse TM, LogicalTransformer).

### Verified white space [re-checked 2026-09-22, log `results/20260922T125436Z_prior_art_check/`; an absence can be searched for, never proven]

- No logic-native model reports an UNCONDITIONAL LM perplexity on a standard corpus (RDDLGN: conditional translation perplexity; QuWeiT: character-level loss on a toy set; see above).
- No TM combined with compositional codes, label trees, or masked diffusion. No TM clause sharding, multi-GPU training, scaling law, or long-context work found.
- NOT white space (cite, do not claim): character-level TM LM (Gorji 2023); TM text generation (Tsetlin.jl); relative-position-tagged context features for per-word TM prediction (LogicalTransformer demos); sparse literal processing (Sparse TM 2405.02375, literal streaming); contextual word prediction as classification features (Abdelwahab, ISTM 2025).

### Open gap that is also a risk
Batch-synchronous summed TA feedback has NO published THEORETICAL analysis. The only published treatment of TM learning with stale class sums is Abeyrathna et al. (ICML 2021, arXiv 2009.04861), which is empirical (no accuracy loss on eight tasks) and whose per-clause update takes a batch size b; torchtsetlin batches without an analysis. Track B's batch semantics (docs/03, docs/11) are therefore measured, not assumed: experiment s3 (docs/05) and kill criterion KB3 (docs/08).
